1 · Why the old names were often wrong

We checked every claim in the old group names (Qwen3-32B, prompt v4) against the music of every group member, on the same samples, for all 106 steps of the three decision trees (316 / 585 / 698).

Key: old estimate vs new estimate vs POP909 annotation

The old key came from note durations in the 16 prefix beats only (Krumhansl–Kessler), which pulls toward a long-held chord (316: the prefix lingers on Ab, so Ab major was chosen instead of Db major). The new key uses no training data: Temperley–Kostka–Payne profile on note onsets, all the music before the cutoff, and the tonic chord the music dwells on to decide major vs relative minor.
