Four ways to group and name the same continuations

1 · Listen and compare

One prefix at a time: its 256 one-bar continuations from AMT, grouped and named by each approach. Click a point or a sample to hear the last prefix bar followed by that continuation; click it again to stop. The panel under the map shows the group of the selected sample under every approach.
How to read a group card
  • Name claims: each checkable statement in the group's name and description (for example "opening chord IV" or "reuses P4"), checked against every sample in the group. The percentage is how many of the group's samples it is true for.
    • green true for most of the group (at least 60% of its samples) and it sets the group apart: at least 20 percentage points more common than in the other groups.
    • amber true for most of the group, but about as common in the other groups, so it does not help you tell the groups apart.
    • red false for most of the group (true for less than 60% of its samples).
  • Members actually (grey): what the group's samples most often do: opening chord, melody, accompaniment.
